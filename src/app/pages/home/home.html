04 — Design language

How a RELIK
piece is drawn.

  1. I

    Sculptural forms

    Every piece starts as a shape before it becomes jewelry. Curves are drawn with intent; edges stay where they belong.

  2. II

    Contrasting textures

    Mirror polish set against rippled, cut and broken surfaces, so light moves across a piece instead of sitting on it.

  3. III

    Substantial silhouettes

    Proportions read from a distance. The goal is presence: pieces that hold their own without competing for it.
